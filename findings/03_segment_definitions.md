# Customer Segmentation Strategy & Campaign Brief

**To:** Head of Marketing  
**From:** Data Analytics  
**Subject:** RFM Customer Segmentation & Quarterly Campaign Target Definitions  

## 1. Executive Summary
To provide clear target groups for upcoming marketing campaigns, we scored and categorized our **8,800 active customers** into **four distinct segments** using quantile-based Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) scoring. 

The analysis reveals extreme revenue concentration: **22.5% of our customer base drives nearly 65.9% of total revenue**, while over 2,360 casual single-purchase buyers account for under 2.5% of spend.

## 3. Segment Behavior & Recommended Campaign Angles

### 1. VIP Champions (22.5% Base | 65.9% Revenue)
* **Behavior Profile:** Highly engaged active buyers purchasing every 12 days on average, with an average lifetime spend of ₹99,315 and over 13 completed orders.
* **Recommended Campaign:** **VIP Exclusivity & Early Access.** Avoid heavy discounting. Instead, protect revenue by offering early product launches, exclusive loyalty perks, and dedicated priority customer support.

### 2. Loyal Regulars (25.4% Base | 17.4% Revenue)
* **Behavior Profile:** Steady repeat shoppers (avg. 3.07 orders) who bought within the last 26 days and spend ~₹23,217 on average.
* **Recommended Campaign:** **Basket Building & Cross-Selling.** Drive order values up using targeted bundle recommendations (e.g., "Complete the Look") and threshold-based perks (e.g., free premium shipping on orders above ₹25,000).

### 3. At-Risk Spenders (25.2% Base | 14.3% Revenue)
* **Behavior Profile:** Previously valuable repeat customers (avg. spend ₹19,256; 2.24 orders) who haven't ordered in over 66 days.
* **Recommended Campaign:** **Re-Engagement Win-Back.** Launch high-urgency win-back triggers featuring limited-time personalized discount incentives (e.g., "We miss you — take ₹1,500 off your next order") to prevent customer churn.

### 4. Casual & One-Timers (26.9% Base | 2.5% Revenue)
* **Behavior Profile:** Single-purchase buyers (avg. frequency strictly 1.00) with low monetary contribution (avg. spend ₹3,135) who haven't bought in ~48 days.
* **Recommended Campaign:** **Automated 2nd-Purchase Nurture.** Deploy automated email/SMS post-purchase sequences featuring product usage tutorials and a time-bound welcome discount for their 2nd order within 14 days of sign-up.
